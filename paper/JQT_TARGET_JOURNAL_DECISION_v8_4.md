# KTES v8.4 Target Journal Decision

**Decision date:** 2026-10-10

## 1. Decision

The recommended first-submission target for the current KTES study is **Journal of Quality Technology**.

The choice is based on scope fit rather than journal metrics. The paper is fundamentally a statistical and computational **test-allocation method** for a severe small-budget quality/reliability setting. Its main contribution is an operational sampling-and-inference architecture that combines active difficult-condition enrichment with a valid unequal-probability basis for finite-population inference.

JQT explicitly seeks methodological development in quality and reliability technology, practical applicability in industry, service, military, and government operations, and innovative use or combination of existing analytical techniques. That scope closely matches c-pKTES-Hedge. JQT also encourages real-data demonstrations and code sharing, which fits the current evidence and reproducibility package.

## 4. JQT-specific positioning

The paper should be submitted as **Regular Research**, not as a case study.

The main storyline is:

> Under a hard physical-test budget, difficult-case discovery and finite-population inference need not be separated into two campaigns. c-pKTES-Hedge embeds a small certainty portfolio inside an unequal-probability design, retaining a large population-inference component while directing tests toward novel and adverse conditions.

The paper should not be positioned as a new general balanced-sampling theorem, a universally superior sampler, a weapon-system certification method, or a retrospective audit diary.

## 5. Submission implications

Because JQT uses double-anonymized review, the review manuscript should not contain an author-identifying public repository link. A blinded repository or private reviewer archive should be used during review, with the public repository and permanent archival identifier restored when permitted.

The four frozen v8.3 SVG figures remain the reproducible scientific masters. The JQT manuscript can reuse them without reopening the scientific analysis.
